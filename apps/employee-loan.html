MODULE OVERVIEW

A loan is more than
a payment to track.

Employee loans create an ongoing financial obligation. The business needs a clear view of requests, approvals, amounts disbursed, installments and what has actually been settled.

01

Loan requests

Capture employee loan requests in a structured record.

02

Approval workflow

Route requests through the configured approval process.

03

Disbursement tracking

Record the loan disbursement and keep it linked to the employee.

04

Installment schedule

Track installments and their payment status.

05

Payroll follow-up

Connect installment follow-up with payroll processing where configured.

06

Outstanding balance

Review paid and remaining amounts from the loan record.

Scope notePayroll deduction and installment scenarios should be validated against the specific release and payroll configuration. Advanced cases such as rescheduling, early settlement and termination settlement are not advertised as confirmed features here.
