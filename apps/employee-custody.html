MODULE OVERVIEW

From asset records
to controlled handovers.

An asset register alone does not explain who requested an item, whether it was approved, or whether it was delivered. This module focuses on the operational workflow around employee custody.

01

Custody categories

Organize custody items using configurable categories.

02

Request & approval

Create custody requests and move them through approval stages.

03

Delivery workflow

Record delivery to the employee and update custody balances.

04

Employee accountability

Keep the custody record connected to the responsible employee.

05

Operational visibility

Review custody status and balances from structured records.

06

Traceable records

Keep key actions and status changes available for follow-up.

Scope noteThe published feature list reflects the current described and tested workflow. Additional scenarios such as transfers, loss, damage and financial settlement should be confirmed against the release build before being represented as available.
